# Voice & Tone

## Voice And Tone

- **Skill Id:** voice-and-tone
- **Brand Name:** ZackAI
### Brand Persona

- **Name:** Zack
- **Archetype:** The Playful Guardian
#### Personality Traits

- Warm and nurturing
- Playfully curious
- Gently encouraging
- Reassuringly calm
- Imaginatively adventurous
- Patient and inclusive
- Trustworthy and transparent
- **Communication Style:** Zack speaks like a beloved older sibling or a favourite storybook narrator — full of wonder, never condescending, always safe. With parents, Zack shifts to a knowledgeable, empathetic ally who respects their values around screen time, multilingualism, and intentional parenting. The voice is simple without being simplistic, warm without being saccharine, and confident without being pushy.
### Voice Attributes

#### Item 1

- **Name:** Warm
- **Definition:** Every word should feel like a cosy blanket — safe, inviting, and full of care.
- **Do:** Use sensory, emotionally resonant language: 'Zack is ready for your next adventure together.'
- **Dont:** Don't use cold, transactional language: 'Device is operational and awaiting input.'
#### Item 2

- **Name:** Playful
- **Definition:** Infuse delight and gentle humour into communication without undermining trust or clarity.
- **Do:** Use light wordplay and curiosity-driven phrasing: 'What if dinosaurs could sing? Zack knows a song about that.'
- **Dont:** Don't force jokes, use sarcasm, or be silly in moments that require reassurance or safety information.
#### Item 3

- **Name:** Reassuring
- **Definition:** Parents need to feel confident that ZackAI is safe, private, and beneficial. Every parent-facing message should radiate trust.
- **Do:** Lead with clarity and evidence: 'No data leaves the device. No ads. No subscriptions. Just learning.'
- **Dont:** Don't be defensive or legalistic: 'Per our GDPR compliance framework, data processing is handled pursuant to Article 6(1)(a).'
#### Item 4

- **Name:** Simple
- **Definition:** Clarity is kindness. Use short sentences, familiar words, and a natural rhythm that works across languages.
- **Do:** Write at a reading age of 10–12 for parent content, 6–8 for child-facing copy: 'Zack speaks 40+ languages — just like your family.'
- **Dont:** Don't use jargon, acronyms without explanation, or complex clause structures: 'Leveraging proprietary NLP to facilitate multilingual pedagogical outcomes.'
#### Item 5

- **Name:** Empowering
- **Definition:** Position the child as the hero of every story. Zack is the sidekick, not the star.
- **Do:** Centre the child's agency: 'You figured it out! Zack knew you could.'
- **Dont:** Don't make the AI the protagonist: 'ZackAI's advanced algorithms taught your child a new concept.'
#### Item 6

- **Name:** Inclusive
- **Definition:** Reflect the diversity of families, languages, and learning styles without tokenism.
- **Do:** Use universal scenarios and gender-neutral defaults: 'Every child learns differently — Zack adapts to yours.'
- **Dont:** Don't assume family structure, language background, or ability: 'Mom and Dad will love watching their son learn English.'
### Tone Spectrum

- **Description:** The core voice stays constant. The tone shifts along a spectrum from most playful to most professional depending on context.
#### Contexts

- **Professionalism:** 3

### Writing Rules

#### Item 1

- **Rule:** Child as hero
- **Description:** Always position the child as the protagonist. Zack is the sidekick, enabler, or companion — never the teacher or authority.
#### Item 2

- **Rule:** One idea per sentence
- **Description:** Keep sentences short and rhythmic. If a sentence has a comma, consider splitting it. Maximum 20 words per sentence in hero copy.
#### Item 3

- **Rule:** Show, don't spec
- **Description:** Never lead with technical specifications. Translate every feature into a felt benefit. '40+ languages' becomes 'speaks their grandmother's language too.'
#### Item 4

- **Rule:** EUR 99 always with context
- **Description:** When mentioning the price, always pair it with the no-subscription promise. Never let the price float alone.
#### Item 5

- **Rule:** Active voice by default
- **Description:** Use active, present-tense constructions. 'Zack tells stories' not 'Stories are told by ZackAI's narrative engine.'
#### Item 6

- **Rule:** Address parents as allies
- **Description:** Never lecture parents. Speak to them as fellow adults who have already made a thoughtful choice. Validate their intention.
#### Item 7

- **Rule:** Safety earns trust, not fear
- **Description:** Present certifications (GDPR, CE, EN71) as proof of care, not as fear-based selling. 'Certified safe because we're parents too' — not 'Protect your child from dangerous toys.'
#### Item 8

- **Rule:** Sensory language for the product
- **Description:** Zack is a physical, huggable object. Use tactile and sensory words: soft, fluffy, squeeze, glow, whisper. Avoid clinical product language.
#### Item 9

- **Rule:** No exclamation point stacking
- **Description:** Maximum one exclamation mark per paragraph. Enthusiasm comes from word choice, not punctuation.
#### Item 10

- **Rule:** Phantom Purple is the signature
- **Description:** When referencing colour in copy, lead with Phantom Purple (#AA98D6). Other variants are secondary. Use colour names, not hex codes, in consumer-facing copy.

### Bilingual Guidelines

- **Primary Market Language:** French (France)
- **Secondary Language:** English
#### Rules

##### Item 1

- **Rule:** French-first for France launch
- **Description:** All consumer-facing copy for the French market must be written natively in French, not translated from English. The brand voice must feel born in French.
##### Item 2

- **Rule:** Tutoiement for child-facing, vouvoiement for parent-facing
- **Description:** Use 'tu' when Zack speaks to children. Use 'vous' when addressing parents in formal contexts (website, email). Social media may use 'tu' for a warmer community feel, at the brand manager's discretion.
##### Item 3

- **Rule:** No anglicisms where French alternatives exist
- **Description:** Avoid 'le learning', 'le screen-free', or 'booster l'engagement'. Use natural French equivalents: 'apprentissage', 'sans ecran', 'stimuler la curiosite'.
##### Item 4

- **Rule:** Brand name stays untranslated
- **Description:** 'ZackAI' and 'Zack' remain unchanged in all languages. Never translate, transliterate, or adapt the brand name.
##### Item 5

- **Rule:** Preserve rhythm and warmth in French
- **Description:** French copy should have the same emotional warmth as the English source. Prioritise feeling over literal accuracy. 'Screen-free, imagination-full' might become 'Sans ecran, plein d'imagination' — not 'Libre d'ecran, complet d'imagination.'
##### Item 6

- **Rule:** Legal and certification terms in local format
- **Description:** Use 'Conforme RGPD' (not 'GDPR compliant'), 'Marquage CE', and 'Norme EN71' in French-market materials.
##### Item 7

- **Rule:** Bilingual packaging
- **Description:** Product packaging and inserts must be bilingual FR/EN. French takes visual priority (larger, first). English follows in a secondary but legible position.
- **French Tone Notes:** French brand copy should feel like a conversation at the parc — natural, unhurried, genuinely caring. Avoid the overly enthusiastic American startup tone. French parents respond to understated confidence, wit, and emotional intelligence. Let the product speak through restraint and charm, not hyperbole.
