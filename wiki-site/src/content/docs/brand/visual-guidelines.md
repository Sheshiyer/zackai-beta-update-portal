## Palette & Typography

- **Primary:** Muted Lilac #AA98D6
- **Secondary:** Cloud White #F8F6F2
- **Accent:** Warm Sunset Orange #E67A2E
- **Header font:** Figtree
- **Body font:** Inter

## Visual Identity Summary

- **Skill Id:** visual-identity-core
- **Brand Name:** ZackAI
- **Tagline:** AI-powered screen-free learning companion for children 3-12
- **Brand Archetype:** The Playful Guardian
### Visual Mood

- **Name:** Soft Intelligence
- **Description:** Approachable AI wrapped in cozy tactile softness. Every visual touchpoint communicates warmth, safety, and gentle curiosity — technology that feels like a hug, not a screen.
#### Keywords

- cozy
- tactile
- gentle
- curious
- plush
- warm
- nurturing
- playful
- organic
- screen-free
- soft-tech
- child-safe
### Color Palette

#### Item 1

- **Name:** Muted Lilac
- **Hex:** #AA98D6
- **Role:** primary
- **Psychology:** Gentle imagination and creative calm. Lilac evokes wonder without overstimulation, signalling a safe space for learning and play. It differentiates ZackAI from the teal-mint territory dominated by competitors.
- **Usage Percentage:** 60
- **Usage:** Hero surfaces, main backgrounds, packaging base, app backgrounds, large UI panels, landing page sections
#### Item 2

- **Name:** Cloud White
- **Hex:** #F8F6F2
- **Role:** secondary
- **Psychology:** Clarity and openness. A warm white that feels clean without sterility, reinforcing the approachable and trustworthy nature of the brand.
- **Usage Percentage:** 30
- **Usage:** Body text backgrounds, card surfaces, clean negative space, typography on dark surfaces, packaging interiors, content containers
#### Item 3

- **Name:** Warm Sunset Orange
- **Hex:** #E67A2E
- **Role:** accent
- **Psychology:** Energy, joy, and action. A warm orange that sparks excitement and draws attention to key moments — buy buttons, achievements, and celebratory interactions.
- **Usage Percentage:** 10
- **Usage:** Call-to-action buttons, interactive highlights, price tags, notification badges, achievement markers, promotional banners
#### Item 4

- **Name:** Soft Almond Cream
- **Hex:** #D9D3C1
- **Role:** support
- **Psychology:** Grounding warmth and tactile comfort. Evokes natural materials — cotton, wood, linen — reinforcing the physical, screen-free nature of the product.
- **Usage:** Borders, dividers, metadata text, subtle backgrounds, secondary UI elements, form fields, tag backgrounds, packaging accents
#### Item 5

- **Name:** Soft Aqua Teal
- **Hex:** #4CC8B2
- **Role:** signal
- **Psychology:** Growth, success, and positive reinforcement. Used sparingly to mark learning milestones and system-healthy states, communicating progress and encouragement.
- **Usage:** Success states, progress indicators, learning milestones, confirmation messages, growth charts, positive feedback animations
### Typography

#### Header

- **Family:** Figtree
##### Weights

- 600 SemiBold
- 700 Bold
- **Fallback:** system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Note:** Closest available match to the brand font Brinnan. Rounded, friendly geometry with strong readability.
##### Sizes

- **H1:** 48px / 3rem
- **H2:** 36px / 2.25rem
- **H3:** 28px / 1.75rem
- **H4:** 22px / 1.375rem
- **Line Height:** 1.2
- **Letter Spacing:** -0.02em
##### Usage Rules

- Use SemiBold (600) for most headings
- Reserve Bold (700) for hero headlines and primary CTAs
- Never use light or thin weights — they undermine the plush brand feel
- Sentence case for all headings, never ALL CAPS
#### Body

- **Family:** Inter
##### Weights

- 400 Regular
- 500 Medium
- **Fallback:** system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
##### Sizes

- **Large:** 18px / 1.125rem
- **Base:** 16px / 1rem
- **Small:** 14px / 0.875rem
- **Caption:** 12px / 0.75rem
- **Line Height:** 1.6
- **Letter Spacing:** 0em
##### Usage Rules

- Regular (400) for body copy and descriptions
- Medium (500) for labels, navigation, and emphasis within text
- Minimum 16px for parent-facing content to ensure readability
- Maintain generous line-height for comfortable reading
#### Data

- **Family:** JetBrains Mono
##### Weights

- 400 Regular
- **Fallback:** monospace
##### Sizes

- **Base:** 14px / 0.875rem
- **Small:** 12px / 0.75rem
- **Line Height:** 1.5
- **Letter Spacing:** 0.02em
##### Usage Rules

- Use exclusively for data displays, stats, prices, and technical specifications
- Never use for body copy or headings
- Pair with Soft Almond Cream backgrounds for data tables
- EUR 99 price display should use this font at Medium weight
### Logo Guidelines

- **Clear Space:** Minimum clear space equal to the height of one ear on all sides of the logo mark
#### Minimum Size

- **Digital:** 32px height
- **Print:** 12mm height
#### Placement

- **Preferred:** Top-left for web, centered for packaging and physical product tags
- **Footer:** Centered with tagline beneath
- **Product:** Embroidered or printed tag on the plush body
#### Color Variations

##### Item 1

- **Name:** Primary
- **Description:** Muted Lilac (#AA98D6) logo mark on Cloud White (#F8F6F2) background
##### Item 2

- **Name:** Reversed
- **Description:** Cloud White (#F8F6F2) logo mark on Muted Lilac (#AA98D6) background
##### Item 3

- **Name:** Accent
- **Description:** Warm Sunset Orange (#E67A2E) logo mark on Cloud White (#F8F6F2) for promotional use
##### Item 4

- **Name:** Mono Dark
- **Description:** Dark gray (#2D2D2D) logo mark for legal and monochrome print
##### Item 5

- **Name:** Mono Light
- **Description:** White (#FFFFFF) logo mark for dark backgrounds and video overlays
#### Prohibited Uses

- Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
- Do not place on busy photographic backgrounds without a container
- Do not change the logo colors outside approved variations
- Do not add drop shadows, gradients, or 3D effects
- Do not place the logo smaller than the minimum size
- Do not recreate the logo in unapproved typefaces
### Imagery Direction

#### Photography Style

- **Description:** Warm, soft-lit lifestyle photography in cozy home environments. Natural light preferred — golden hour, window light, soft diffused daylight. Shallow depth of field to create a dreamy, gentle feel.
- **Color Treatment:** Slightly warm white balance, soft contrast, gentle highlight roll-off. Never harsh or clinical.
- **Composition:** Eye-level or child's-eye-level perspective. Close-up and medium shots emphasising tactile interaction with the plush.
#### Illustration Style

- **Description:** Rounded, organic vector illustrations with soft edges and gentle gradients. Characters and objects should feel inflated and plush, as if they could be squeezed.
- **Line Weight:** Thick, rounded strokes (3-4px at standard sizes). No sharp corners.
- **Color Application:** Flat fills from the brand palette with subtle soft shadows. No hard drop shadows.
#### Environments

- Cozy children's bedrooms with soft rugs and cushions
- Sunny living rooms with natural wood and fabric textures
- Garden and outdoor settings with soft grass and dappled light
- Reading nooks and pillow forts
- Kitchen tables during creative play
#### Constraints

- No children's faces shown — focus on hands, backs, silhouettes
- Hands-and-toy focus: always show physical interaction with the ZackAI plush
- No screens visible in any imagery — this is a screen-free product
- No cold, sterile, or laboratory-like environments
- No isolated product shots on plain white — always contextualize in a warm setting
### Graphic Elements

#### Shapes

- **Style:** Rounded and organic
- **Corner Radius:** 16px minimum for UI elements, fully rounded for badges and tags
- **Description:** All shapes should feel soft and approachable. Prefer rounded rectangles, circles, and blob-like organic forms. No sharp angles or pointed geometric shapes.
#### Textures

- **Style:** Soft and fluffy
##### Types

- Subtle fabric/felt texture overlays at low opacity
- Soft noise grain for depth on flat surfaces
- Cotton-like cloud textures for background elements
- Gentle linen patterns for supporting surfaces
- **Application:** Use sparingly to add tactile warmth. Never overwhelming — the palette should remain dominant.
#### Patterns

##### Item 1

- **Name:** Ear Scatter
- **Description:** Repeating small ZackAI ear silhouettes in Soft Almond Cream on Cloud White, used for wrapping paper, box liners, and background fills
##### Item 2

- **Name:** Dot Grid
- **Description:** Soft rounded dots in Muted Lilac at 10% opacity, used for empty states and secondary backgrounds
##### Item 3

- **Name:** Wave Lines
- **Description:** Gentle sine-wave lines in Soft Almond Cream, evoking sound waves and learning signals
#### Icon Style

- **Stroke Weight:** 2.5px rounded stroke
- **Corners:** Fully rounded caps and joins
- **Style:** Thick, friendly, rounded line icons. Consistent 24x24 grid.
- **Fill:** No fills — outline only. Use brand palette colors.
- **Description:** Icons should feel chunky and approachable, like they were drawn with a soft marker. Never thin, sharp, or overly detailed.
### Sigil

- **Description:** Round fluffy creature silhouette with pointed cat-like ears and glowing LED eyes
- **Form:** A compact, circular body shape — like a soft ball or mochi — topped with two pointed triangular cat ears. The silhouette should be instantly recognizable even at small sizes.
- **Eyes:** Two simple oval or rounded-rectangle LED eyes that glow with Soft Aqua Teal (#4CC8B2). The eyes convey emotion and state — happy, listening, thinking, sleeping.
- **Texture:** The body should communicate fluffiness through soft edge treatment — slightly fuzzy or feathered outlines rather than hard vector edges.
#### Usage

- Favicon and app icon (simplified to ears + eyes only)
- Loading and transition animations
- Watermark on brand documents
- Social media profile image
- Packaging seal and sticker
#### Eye States

##### Item 1

- **State:** happy
- **Description:** Curved upward crescents, glowing teal
##### Item 2

- **State:** listening
- **Description:** Wide open ovals, pulsing gently
##### Item 3

- **State:** thinking
- **Description:** Slightly squinted, slow color shift
##### Item 4

- **State:** sleeping
- **Description:** Thin horizontal lines, dim glow
##### Item 5

- **State:** celebrating
- **Description:** Star-shaped sparkle eyes, bright orange accent
### Negative Prompt

- **Description:** Elements to explicitly avoid in all visual generation for ZackAI
#### Avoid

- Metallic or chrome surfaces
- Sharp geometric angles and aggressive shapes
- Dark or moody color schemes
- Neon or electric colors
- Robot or humanoid AI imagery
- Screens, tablets, phones, or any digital device displays
- Photorealistic children's faces
- Cold blue tech lighting
- Circuit board patterns or binary code visuals
- Corporate or enterprise aesthetics
- Cluttered or overstimulating compositions
- Teal or mint as a primary color (competitor territory)
- Poppins typeface
- Glossy plastic or hard-tech materials
- Gradients using blue-to-purple (too generic tech)
- Stock photo aesthetics — forced smiles, white backgrounds
- Any imagery suggesting screen time or digital addiction
### Competitor Differentiation

#### Visual Territory Owned

- **Color:** Muted Lilac as primary — a distinctive, warm-imaginative space no competitor occupies. Lilac signals gentle creativity vs. the clinical trust of teal/blue.
- **Texture:** Plush, fluffy, tactile softness — ZackAI owns the 'huggable tech' visual territory.
- **Personality:** The Playful Guardian — protective yet fun, wise yet silly. Visually expressed through rounded, cushioned forms and warm lighting.
- **Typography:** Figtree's rounded geometry feels handmade and approachable vs. the generic modernism of Poppins.
#### Visual Territory Avoided

##### Item 1

- **Competitor:** Bondu
- **Their Territory:** Teal/mint (#78C9B3) primary palette, Poppins typography
- **Our Differentiation:** Muted Lilac primary avoids any teal overlap. Figtree and Inter replace Poppins.
##### Item 2

- **Competitor:** Generic EdTech
- **Their Territory:** Cold tech aesthetics, blue gradients, screen-centric imagery
- **Our Differentiation:** Warm, tactile, screen-free imagery. No device screens ever shown. Technology is invisible — the magic lives in the plush.
##### Item 3

- **Competitor:** Toy Robots
- **Their Territory:** Hard plastic, metallic surfaces, humanoid robot forms
- **Our Differentiation:** Soft, organic, fluffy creature. No hard edges, no metal, no robot joints. ZackAI is a companion, not a gadget.
