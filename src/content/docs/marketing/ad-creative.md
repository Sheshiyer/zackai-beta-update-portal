# Ad Creative

## Pre Launch Ads

### Meta

- **Brand:** ZackAI
- **Launch Channel:** kickstarter
- **Generated By:** craft-agent-fixture-completion
- **Quality Bar:** premium
- **Skill:** pre-launch-ads
- **Narrative:** Pre-launch ads should build waitlist energy around the idea that ZackAI offers a safer, warmer, more design-forward alternative to screen-based childhood tech.
### Handoff

#### Audience Segments

- intentional parents
- bilingual households
- Montessori-minded families
- premium toy buyers
#### Hooks

- A screen-free AI friend for curious kids
- The plush companion that speaks 40+ languages
- Bedtime stories with emotional intelligence built in
#### Headline Variants

- Screen-Free Playtime Companion
- Warm intelligence for growing minds
- A smarter playtime companion—without screens
#### Body Copy Variants

- ZackAI brings conversational learning into a soft, glowing plush companion parents can trust.
- Designed for playful minds, bedtime rituals, and multilingual homes—without subscriptions or screen dependency.
#### Cta Variants

- Join the waitlist
- Get early access
- Be first on Kickstarter
#### Creative Direction

- warm natural light
- hands-only child interaction
- hero plush in Muted Lilac
- soft premium textures
#### Angles To Test

- screen-free learning
- emotional intelligence
- multilingual play
- parent trust

## Live Campaign Ads

### Meta

- **Brand:** ZackAI
- **Launch Channel:** kickstarter
- **Generated By:** craft-agent-fixture-completion
- **Quality Bar:** premium
- **Skill:** live-campaign-ads
- **Narrative:** Live ads should shift from promise-first messaging to momentum-first messaging, showing that families are already backing ZackAI and there is still time to join.
### Handoff

#### Campaign Stage Segments

- launch surge
- mid-campaign trust
- final urgency
#### Hooks

- Back the plush AI families are rallying behind
- Still time to join the screen-free movement
- The warm, multilingual companion now live on Kickstarter
#### Copy Variants

- Families are backing ZackAI because it feels like the future of play—soft, safe, and screen-free.
- Backers are joining for the warmth, staying for the multilingual intelligence, and sharing it for the peace of mind.
#### Urgency Messages

- Early tiers are moving
- Launch pricing won’t last
- Join the first wave before campaign close
#### Retargeting Angles

- waitlist visitors
- video viewers
- email clickers
- reward-page dwellers
#### Creative Guidance

- use real campaign progress
- highlight reward tiers
- show glowing eyes and hands-only interaction
