### Persona Profile

<div class="table-scroll">

| Attribute | Details | Marketing Implications |
|---|---|---|
| **Demographics** | Age 30-42, upper-middle income, urban France / expat families | Targets a research-driven segment that values premium educational tools |
| **Psychographics** | Intentional parenting, multilingualism, emotional intelligence | Lead with "Playful Guardian" archetype — technology that feels like a hug |
| **Frustrations** | Screen-time guilt, lack of French-language smart toys, long bedtime routines | Position ZackAI as relief from passive screen consumption |
| **Trust and Safety** | GDPR-first architecture, CE/EN71 certifications, parent-only app controls | Safety as competitive moat — contrast with smart speaker privacy issues |
